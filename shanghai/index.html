What to Expect

Things Not Included in the Itinerary

Anything not mentioned in the itinerary is paid by customers — e.g. the Disneyland Express Pass or souvenirs.

Large Crowds

Expect large crowds and queues in Shanghai, especially during holidays and peak travel seasons.

Communication & Payment

Download WeChat and a translation app before your trip, and set up cashless payment — some places prefer it.

Moderate Walking

Some walking is required at Disneyland and around the city — pack comfortable shoes.

Drop-off & Pickup Point

The drop-off point may not be directly at the entrance, so some walking may be needed.
